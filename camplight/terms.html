What Camplight is — and what it is not

Camplight is a focus timer wrapped in an ambient campsite scene. You choose how long to concentrate, the campfire burns while you work, and the app keeps a record of the sessions you finished.

It is not a medical, therapeutic or psychological service, and it does not treat, diagnose or manage any condition, including attention disorders or phone addiction. If focus or screen use is affecting your health, please talk to a professional rather than to a timer.

Strict mode is a rule that lives inside Camplight, not a restriction on your phone: if you leave the app during a session, the session fails and the fire goes out. Nothing stops you from leaving.

Locked Mode can go further, but only if you ask it to. If you turn on app blocking and grant Camplight access to Screen Time, the apps you picked yourself become unavailable for the length of the session. You choose them every time, Camplight never picks them for you, and it never touches an app you did not select. Blocking ends when the session does.

This is not an unbreakable lock, and we would rather you did not believe it is. Screen Time permission can be withdrawn in Settings on your iPhone in a few steps, and withdrawing it ends the blocking at once. Camplight cannot prevent that, cannot restrict anything you have not chosen, and has no power over how you use your phone outside a session.

Free and paid

These parts of Camplight are free and will stay free:

  • the forest clearing campsite, day and night;
  • the stopwatch, with strict mode;
  • your streaks, your records and your total focus time;
  • daily reminders;
  • two music tracks and the full nature soundscape.

A subscription unlocks the rest:

  • every campsite, including the ones added in future updates;
  • the whole music library and its themed sets;
  • Locked Mode, the stricter session where leaving the app always ends it;
  • blocking the apps you choose for the length of a Locked Mode session;
  • detailed statistics.
