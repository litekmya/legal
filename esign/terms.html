What eSign is — and what it is not

eSign lets you fill in and sign PDF files and paper documents on your iPhone or iPad: you open or scan a document, place your signature, initials, a date or text on it, fill in its form fields and export the result.

eSign creates simple electronic signatures. A simple electronic signature is accepted for many everyday documents in many countries — for example under the ESIGN Act and UETA in the United States and as a simple electronic signature under eIDAS in the European Union. It is not an advanced or qualified electronic signature, it does not verify anyone's identity, and it does not add a digital certificate to the file.

Some documents need more than that: notarised documents, wills, some property and court papers, and anything the other party says must be signed in another way. It is your responsibility to check that a simple electronic signature is acceptable for your document. eSign is not a legal service and does not give legal advice.

eSign does not send documents to other people for signature and does not keep an audit trail. The signed file is exactly what you export.
