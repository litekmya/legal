Support

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This game is made by one person. Write to tekmyaworkplace@gmail.com and you will get an answer from that person, usually within two or three days.

The easiest way is Write to us in the app's Settings: the email it opens already says which iPhone, iOS version, app version and language you have. Just tell us what happened and what you expected.

How do I play?

Tap How to play on the home screen for the rules in four steps. In short: split into teams, pass the phone, tap to see a card and name three things that fit before five seconds run out. The group decides whether the answers count.

What is in the Spicy 18+ pack?

Cards about flirting, first dates, relationships and awkward romantic moments — cheeky, never explicit. It is for adults only: the first time you open it, the app asks whether you are 18 or older. The pack is marked with 🌶️ everywhere, and it is only played if you choose it, so it won't show up by accident at a family game night.

What is in the No Filter 18+ pack?

Bold, brutally honest cards: secrets, gossip, small acts of revenge, cringe moments and the things nobody admits out loud — cheeky, never explicit. It is for adults only and uses the same 18+ question and 🌶️ mark as the Spicy pack.

Does everyone need the app?

No. The whole group plays on one iPhone, passed from team to team. Up to 10 teams can play.

Do my friends need to buy packs too?

No. Packs are unlocked on the phone you play on, and everyone at the table plays them.

The same cards keep coming up

They shouldn't: the game remembers which cards this phone has already shown and only repeats them after the whole pack has been played. To start a pack over, use Settings → Reset played cards.

How do I change the language?

In the app: Settings → Language. It switches straight away, cards included.

How do I get my purchases back?

Tap Restore purchases on the Packs screen or in Settings. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they come back on any iPhone signed in with the same one, at no extra cost.

How do I cancel the weekly subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → 5 Second Rule → Cancel Subscription. Do it at least 24 hours before it renews. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
