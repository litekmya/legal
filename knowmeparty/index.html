Last updated: 24 September 2026

Legal and support pages for How Well Do You Know Me: Group, a party game for 2 to 6 players, each on their own iPhone.

  • Privacy Policy — what the game does with your name and answers. The short version: they stay in the room.
  • Terms of Use — the licence, what is free, how the packs and the weekly subscription work.
  • Support — common questions and how to reach a human.

In short

The game has no server and no accounts. Phones in the same room talk to each other directly, and answers are not saved after the game. There is no advertising and no tracking. Purchases are handled by Apple, with RevenueCat keeping track of which packs are unlocked and a few anonymous game counters, such as how many players were in a game.
