How Well Do You Know Me: Party

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

In short: the game has no server and no accounts. Your name and answers travel only between the phones in your room and are not stored after the game. The developer never sees them. The one outside service the app talks to is RevenueCat, which handles in-app purchases.

What you enter

  • Your player name and colour. You choose them on first launch and can change them any time.
  • Your answers during a game. Guesses about the other players and answers about yourself.

That is all. The app does not ask for your email, phone number, contacts, photos, location or microphone.

Where it is stored

Your name, colour, chosen language, sound setting and the list of questions this phone has already shown are saved only on your phone, in the app's own storage. Answers are kept in memory for the length of a game and are never written to disk.

If iPhone backup is turned on, the app's small settings file is included in your backup to iCloud or your computer, like any other app's settings. Apple operates those backups; the developer has no access to them.

Playing with other people

The game connects phones in the same room directly to each other over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity. Nothing passes through the internet or any server. The connection is encrypted.

To play, the phones exchange what the game needs: each player's name and colour, and the answers given during the round. Other players in your room will see your name and your answers — that is the game. When the game ends, the other phones do not keep them.

iOS will ask you for Local Network access (and may mention Bluetooth). It is used only to find the other phones in the room.

When data leaves your phone because you chose to

You can share an invitation to the game or a picture of the results using the standard iOS share sheet. You pick where it goes — Messages, a chat app, social media. Once sent, that service's own rules apply. The app does not send anything on its own.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your name, Apple ID, card or payment details.

To know which packs you have unlocked, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID created by the app, the details of your purchases (which product, when, in which country's store, the subscription status) and basic technical details such as the app version and iOS version. Like any online service, it sees the IP address of the request. It does not receive your name, email, answers or anything you type in the game.

No ads, analytics or tracking

The app shows no ads, contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools, does not track you across other apps or websites and does not access your device's advertising identifier.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from them. As described above, the app itself collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: delete the app and everything it saved is removed. Copies inside an older iPhone backup stay there until that backup is replaced or deleted.
  • At RevenueCat: email us and we will ask RevenueCat to delete the record for your anonymous ID. Your purchase history with Apple itself stays with Apple.
  • Things you shared: pictures or invitations you sent to other apps are controlled by those apps.

Changes to this policy

If a future version starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
