Support

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This game is made by one person. Write to tekmyaworkplace@gmail.com and you will get an answer from that person, usually within two or three days.

The easiest way is Write to us in the app's Settings: the email it opens already says which iPhone, iOS version, app version and language you have. Just tell us what happened and what you expected.

How do I play?

Tap How to play on the home screen for the rules in four steps. In short: one player answers a question about themselves, everyone else guesses the answer, and it opens on every phone at the same second.

Does everyone need the app?

Yes. Each player uses their own iPhone with the app installed, and everyone needs to be in the same room.

The phones can't find each other

Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on every phone. They don't need to be on the same network. Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and make sure the game is allowed. Guest or hotel Wi-Fi sometimes stops devices from seeing each other. If that happens, switch Wi-Fi off and on and try again.

Do my friends need to buy packs too?

No. Only the host needs a pack. Everyone in the room plays the packs unlocked on the host's phone.

How do I change the language?

In the app: Settings → Language. It switches straight away. The whole room plays in the host's language, because the host's phone sends the questions.

What is Mix mode?

A game of random questions from all packs. It opens with Forever, with the weekly subscription, or once every pack is bought.

How do I get my purchases back?

Open Question packs in the app (on the home screen or in Settings) and tap Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they come back on any iPhone signed in with the same one, at no extra cost.

How do I cancel the weekly subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → How Well Do You Know Me: Group → Cancel Subscription. Do it at least 24 hours before it renews. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
