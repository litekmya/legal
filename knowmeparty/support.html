Support

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Squadoo is made by one person. Write to tekmyaworkplace@gmail.com and you will get an answer from that person, usually within two or three days.

The easiest way is Write to us in the app's Settings: the email it opens already says which iPhone, iOS version, app version and language you have. Just tell us what happened and what you expected.

How do I play?

Choose a game on the home screen and tap How to play for the rules in four steps. In Who Knows Who one player answers a question about themselves, everyone else guesses, and the answer opens on every phone at the same second. In Quick Three a category appears, the clock starts, and a team names three things before it runs out.

Does everyone need the app?

For Who Knows Who, yes: each player uses their own iPhone with the app installed, and everyone needs to be in the same room. Quick Three needs only one phone, which you pass to the team whose turn it is.

The phones can't find each other

Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on every phone. They don't need to be on the same network. Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and make sure the app is allowed. Guest or hotel Wi-Fi sometimes stops devices from seeing each other. If that happens, switch Wi-Fi off and on and try again.

Do my friends need to buy packs too?

No. In Who Knows Who only the host needs a pack. Everyone in the room plays the packs unlocked on the host's phone.

How do I change the language?

In the app: Settings → Language. It switches straight away. In Who Knows Who the whole room plays in the host's language, because the host's phone sends the questions.

What is Mix mode?

A Who Knows Who game of random questions from your packs. It opens with an active pass, with All Current Packs, or once every paid pack is bought.

Where are the 18+ packs?

Four Quick Three packs are for adults. Open Settings, turn on Show 18+ packs and confirm your age. You can turn them off again in the same place.

How do I get my purchases back?

Open Question packs in the app (on the home screen or in Settings) and tap Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they come back on any iPhone signed in with the same one, at no extra cost.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Squadoo → Cancel Subscription. Do it at least 24 hours before it renews. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
