What Speed Reading is — and what it is not

Speed Reading is a reading trainer. It shows text one word at a time at a speed you choose (RSVP), measures your reading speed, checks comprehension with short questions and offers a daily training program with a Schulte table exercise.

Results differ from person to person. The speed forecast after the test is an estimate based on daily practice, not a promise. Reading faster can lower comprehension; the app checks understanding so you can find a speed that works for you.

Speed Reading is not a medical, psychological or educational assessment. The comparison with other readers is an estimate, not a clinical test, and the app does not diagnose or treat any condition, including ADHD or dyslexia.

Text recognition from scans and photos is automatic and can make mistakes, especially with handwriting, poor light or unusual layouts. Pages behind a login or a paywall may not be imported from a link.
